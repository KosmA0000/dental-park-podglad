WKLEJ PONIZSZE:

Soft hand-painted illustration in the style of a calm misty morning, flat top-down orthographic view, gouache and watercolor texture with visible brush grain, gentle painterly shading and no hard outlines, muted desaturated natural palette, minimal and uncluttered. Use only these colors: deep forest green #1a3631, sage green #8b9a86, pale haze #ced2be, warm sand #d6cdaa, brass #8a6f39, cream #f2f1e8. Stylized, not photorealistic. No 3D render. No cartoon black outlines.

Dappled shadow cast by sparse tree foliage, seen from directly above on flat ground. Show ONLY the shadow shapes themselves — not the leaves, not the ground, not the tree. The shadow is painted in a single flat dark tone with soft blurred edges, forming irregular organic patches with gaps of open light between them. Roughly half the frame is shadow, half is empty.

CRITICAL: the image must tile seamlessly in both directions — the top edge matches the bottom edge, and the left edge matches the right edge, with shadow shapes running off the edges and continuing on the opposite side.

Everything that is not shadow is transparent.

Do not include: black outlines, cel shading, photorealism, 3D render, perspective or camera tilt, cast shadows, drop shadows, vignette, text, watermark, signature, frame or border, white background, grass on the gravel, people, buildings.
